In the fast-paced world of crypto trading, knowing what the best traders are doing can give you a serious edge. That’s where [CORESIGHT](https://coresightbot.vercel.app/), comes in. CORESIGHT helps you discover the most profitable wallets and analyze their performance across multiple chains. Whether you’re trading on Solana or EVM-based chains, this tool puts valuable blockchain data right in your Telegram chat.

In this guide, we’ll show you how CORESIGHT works and how you can use it to track successful crypto traders.


## What is CORESIGHT?

![Coresight](/assets/images/coresight.jpg)

CORESIGHT is a Telegram bot designed to make blockchain analytics simple and accessible. By leveraging [Dune Analytics](https://dune.com) data, it identifies the most profitable traders for specific tokens and provides profit/loss (PnL) analysis for wallets. It supports **Solana** and **13+ EVM-compatible chains**, giving you a wide view of on-chain activity.

Whether you’re tracking token holders or analyzing trader performance, CORESIGHT delivers actionable insights directly to your phone.


## Key Features

CORESIGHT offers a powerful set of features designed to help you identify and analyze the most successful crypto traders across multiple chains. With its *Solana Token Wallet Finder*, you can easily discover the top-performing traders holding specific Solana tokens. To go even deeper, the *Solana Wallet PnL Analysis* lets you check the profit and loss of any Solana wallet over the past 30 days, giving you valuable insight into individual trading performance.

![Coresight 4](/assets/images/coresight4.png)

For those trading on Ethereum or other EVM-compatible chains like BNB Chain and Polygon, CORESIGHT’s *EVM Token Wallet Finder* identifies the most profitable wallets across your chosen tokens. You can also analyze wallet performance directly with the *EVM Wallet PnL Analysis*, making it simple to see how well traders are doing on these networks.

Beyond tracking profitability, CORESIGHT includes an *Inspect Tool* powered by [Webacy](https://x.com/mywebacy). This feature enhances your analysis with wallet security assessments and risk insights, helping you avoid suspicious or unsafe wallets.

The bot also supports multi-chain analytics, allowing you to track wallets and tokens across Solana and more than 13 EVM chains. For those who prefer working with raw data, CORESIGHT provides CSV export options, so you can download detailed wallet and token reports for further analysis.

Together, these tools make CORESIGHT an essential companion for anyone looking to follow winning traders and refine their crypto strategy.


## Getting Started with CORESIGHT

Getting started with CORESIGHT is simple and takes just a few minutes. To begin, open the Telegram app and search for `@coresight_bot`. Once you’ve found it, click on the bot and press the Start button to activate it. To unlock all of CORESIGHT’s powerful features, you’ll need to subscribe to one of its available plans.

![Coresight 2](/assets/images/coresight2.png)

After setting up your subscription, you can also join the CORESIGHT community chat to stay up to date with the latest updates, tips, and support from other users.

*Keep in mind that CORESIGHT requires an active subscription to access its analytics tools.*


## Core Commands You Should Know

Once you’ve set up CORESIGHT, the next step is to get familiar with its core commands. These simple yet powerful commands let you navigate the bot’s features with ease, whether you’re tracking wallet performance, analyzing tokens, or performing advanced security checks. Here’s a quick reference guide to help you get started:

| Command             | Description                                                   |
|---------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| `/start`            | Initialize the bot and get a welcome message                 |
| `/subscribe`        | Purchase a subscription plan                                  |
| `/subscription`     | Check your active subscription status                         |
| `/help`             | See all available commands and their details                  |
| `/cabal`            | Find Solana token wallets                                     |
| `/walletpnl`        | View 30-day PnL for Solana wallets                            |
| `/evmcabal`         | Discover EVM token wallets                                    |
| `/evmwalletpnl`     | Analyze EVM wallet PnL                                        |
| `/inspect`          | Perform advanced wallet security analysis                     |

By mastering these commands, you’ll have full control over CORESIGHT’s analytics tools. From identifying profitable wallets to assessing wallet security, these shortcuts make it easy to explore blockchain data directly from your Telegram chat.  

*Tip: Use the `/help` command anytime to see detailed instructions for all available features.*
